Fair play is mandatory

Know the rules before you enter.

Strict competition rules help protect the integrity of every round. Final rules will be shown before a tournament starts.

01
One question at a timeOnce submitted, an answer is final. You cannot return to earlier questions.
02
No switching tabsDetected attempts to leave the active quiz may trigger immediate disqualification and a ban from future EJC Trivia Arena competitions.
03
No refreshing during a live roundRefreshing or reloading may end your attempt. Keep the quiz open.
04
Stable connection requiredConnection loss can interrupt participation. Join with a reliable internet connection.
05
No outside assistanceSearch engines, AI tools, messaging apps and help from other people are prohibited during a round.
06
Monitoring has limitsA website cannot reliably detect every screenshot, second device or use of AI. Suspicious activity may require review.
